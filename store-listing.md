## Full description
FocusForge is a Pomodoro and study timer that keeps it simple: start a
focus session, take a proper break, and see where your time actually went.

- Custom focus, short break and long break lengths
- Tasks linked to sessions, with per-task focus totals
- Session history, day streaks and a 7-day focus chart
- Daily focus goal with a progress bar
- Sound and vibration alerts when a session ends
- Your timer survives the app being closed — it resumes where it left off
- Auto-start breaks and focus sessions if you want a hands-free flow
- Light and dark themes

Everything is stored on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking,
no internet needed.
